About Me

I'm Kevlon Galloway, a full-stack developer based in Atlanta, Georgia with over a decade of experience building web applications that actually ship — from high-traffic national platforms to tightly scoped client sites.

My background is rooted in backend engineering — system architecture, APIs, databases, and the infrastructure that keeps things running. But I'm equally at home on the frontend, building interfaces that are fast, clean, and built to convert.

While at Telrite Holdings, I contributed to PureTalk and Life Wireless — two live production platforms serving real customers at national scale. Alongside that, I founded Nolvek, a digital agency helping local Georgia businesses establish and grow their online presence.

I build lean, I ship fast, and I don't waste your time. If you need someone who can own a project end-to-end — let's talk.
